CASE 11 Fraud: 1 fraudster per 1,000 honest users Datacore · anti-fraud team
1 The situation

The model you inherited reports 99% accuracy, and the team is celebrating. Predicting “not fraud” every single time would score 99.9%. The metric is lying to everyone in the room.

2 Your ML decisions

Choose metrics that survive a 1:1000 imbalance — precision, recall, ROC-AUC. Weight the classes or resample. Then move the decision threshold on purpose instead of leaving it at 0.5.

3 Business impact

Every threshold trades blocked honest customers against fraud that gets through. You pick a number, and you defend it — exactly the way an interviewer will ask you to.
