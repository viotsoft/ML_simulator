CASE 11 · ANTI-FRAUD Fraud: 1 fraudster per 1,000 honest users
1 The situation

The model you inherited reports 99% accuracy. Predicting “not fraud” every time would score 99.9%. The metric is lying to everyone in the room.

2 Your ML decisions

Pick metrics that survive a 1:1000 imbalance — precision, recall, ROC-AUC. Weight the classes. Move the threshold on purpose, not by default.

3 Business impact

Every threshold trades blocked honest customers against fraud that gets through. You pick a number and defend it — as an interviewer will ask you to.
